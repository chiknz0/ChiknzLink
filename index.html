Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes! Our Free Wing plan allows up to 10 links per month and 1 custom domain at absolutely no cost.

What is Pro Bucket?

Pro Bucket is our premium plan. For $6.99/mo, you get 200 links per month, 3 custom domains, and advanced analytics.

Do links expire?

No, your links stay active permanently as long as the ChiknzLink service is running.

How do Custom Domains work?

You can point your own domain (e.g., links.yourbrand.com) to our IP addresses to maintain your brand identity on every link you share.
